Security & Data Governance

Privacy Policy & AI Data Protection

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 · Last Updated: October 2026 · Version 2.4

Core AI Data Principle: Zero Foundation Model Training

GPTify OS adheres to strict enterprise data isolation. Your operational data, patient records, customer messages, voice notes, and CRM records are NEVER used to train, retrain, or improve public foundation models (including Microsoft Azure OpenAI, Google Cloud Gemini, or Anthropic Claude). Your proprietary data remains 100% yours and is strictly partitioned by tenant Row-Level Security (RLS).

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how GPTify Technologies MChJ ("GPTify", "we", "us", or "our"), operating the GPTify OS platform (including Ailo, Bandla, ILDAM, Mirzo, Izlai, and vertical fleets MedPro²⁴, EduPro²⁴, and AutoPro²⁴ at https://os.gptify.co), collects, processes, stores, and protects data when businesses and their end-users access our autonomous operating software, Telegram Mini Apps, APIs, and communication webhooks.

2. Information We Process

We process information under two distinct roles:

  • Customer Account Data (Data Controller): Business contact details, tenant registration credentials, billing information, and administrative account logs required to maintain enterprise subscriptions.
  • Operational Business Data (Data Processor): Customer service messages submitted through connected channels (Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram Direct), appointment calendar requests, patient consultation notes, student tuition logs, and voice recordings processed on behalf of our enterprise business clients.

3. Purpose of Processing

Operational data is processed strictly for the automated delivery of authorized platform workflows:

  • Routing incoming customer inquiries through the Ailo triage agent;
  • Checking real-time provider calendars and locking appointment slots via Bandla;
  • Creating and updating structured customer records in ILDAM CRM;
  • Transcribing audio consultations into structured clinical or meeting summaries via Mirzo;
  • Issuing local payment links and automated fiscal receipts via Click, Payme, and Uzum APIs.

4. Technical Security & Encryption Architecture

We implement defense-in-depth security standards across all layers of the GPTify OS infrastructure:

  • Encryption in Transit: All HTTP API traffic, Telegram webhook updates, and database connections are enforced using TLS 1.3 with modern cipher suites.
  • Encryption at Rest: Database tables, encrypted vector stores (pgvector), and telemetry logs are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Multi-Tenant Data Isolation: Every enterprise tenant's database records are segregated using PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS), preventing cross-tenant leakage.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Operational staff access is governed by strict least-privilege principles, multi-factor authentication (MFA), and immutable audit trails.

5. Third-Party AI Cloud Sub-processors

To deliver low-latency intelligence, GPTify OS routes inference tasks to enterprise cloud providers under strict Enterprise Data Protection Agreements (DPAs):

  • Microsoft Azure: Azure OpenAI Service (GPT-4o / GPT-4o-mini) deployed in dedicated enterprise regions with zero-data-logging agreements.
  • Google Cloud Platform: Vertex AI (Gemini 2.0 Flash / Pro) utilized for sub-second classification and multimodal audio inference.
  • Cloudflare: Global Edge CDN, DDoS mitigation, and SSL termination.

None of these sub-processors have rights to retain or inspect customer operational payloads outside the execution window.

6. Meta Platform Data (Instagram & WhatsApp) & Deletion Requests

For inquiries handled via Instagram Direct or WhatsApp Business APIs through our Ailo engine, we process only the message text, sender identifier, and interaction timestamp required to fulfill the user's booking or inquiry request.

User Data Deletion Instructions: Any end-user who has interacted with a business powered by GPTify OS on Instagram or WhatsApp may request the deletion of their conversation history by submitting an email to privacy@gptify.co with their username or telephone number. All associated conversation logs will be permanently purged within 7 business days.

7. Healthcare (HIPAA-Aligned) and Education Data Alignment

For clinics deploying MedPro²⁴, patient medical charts, 32-tooth dental maps, and prescription histories are stored in dedicated healthcare-isolated schemas. We do not sell or monetize health or educational data under any circumstances.

8. Regulatory Compliance

GPTify OS operates in full compliance with the Republic of Uzbekistan Law No. ZRU-547 "On Personal Data", international GDPR principles for cross-border data transfer, and applicable local digital economy frameworks.
