Our method

How we work

We review and simplify how your business runs before we build anything. First we map where time, enquiries and bookings are lost. Then we design the process so every reminder and hand-off has a clear purpose. Only then do we connect the tools, test them end to end and train your team.

Your data

How we handle your data

Everything runs on your own cloud accounts or servers. We use the official Meta APIs, encrypted databases and strict access controls. Your client data stays yours and is never used to train AI models. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

The wider GPTify

What else we do

GPTify also publishes AI Insights for Sales & Marketing, a weekly newsletter, runs GTM Shelf, a directory of AI tools for sales and marketing teams, and trains teams in Uzbekistan through GPTify Uzbekistan. We build custom AI agents with our partner Cubeo AI.
