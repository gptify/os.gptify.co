The problem

Where clinics lose patients today.

Evening messages wait until morning

Patients write on Instagram and Telegram after work. By the time reception replies, many have booked somewhere else.

Reception is on the phone all day

Staff answer the same questions about prices, doctors and opening hours instead of looking after patients at the desk.

Empty chairs from no-shows

Without a reminder, booked patients forget, and the doctor’s time is lost.

Records in notebooks and chats

Patient details are spread across paper, phones and messengers, so nobody has the full picture.

Questions

What clinics ask first.

Will patients know they are talking to an assistant?

Yes. Ailo introduces itself as the clinic’s automated assistant, and your staff can take over any conversation at any time.

What happens with medical questions?

Ailo does not give medical advice. Questions about symptoms, test results or treatment, and any complaint, are passed straight to a member of staff.

Which languages does it speak?

Uzbek and Russian. It replies in the language the patient writes in.

Does it work with our doctors’ schedules?

Yes. Bandla connects to Google Calendar, Outlook and your staff shifts. It checks real availability before confirming a slot and sends reminders to reduce no-shows.

How long does setup take?

The core setup takes from 48 hours to 5 business days. After that we train your team live, give you video guides and monitor the system for the first 14 days.

How is patient data protected?

Everything runs on accounts and servers agreed with your clinic, with encrypted databases and strict access controls. Patient data stays yours and is never used to train AI models.
