Questions

What people ask first.

How do the five tools work together?
They form one loop: Izlai finds and contacts new clients, Ailo answers enquiries within 6 seconds, ILDAM tracks the relationship and payments, Bandla books the appointment and Mirzo records what was agreed in meetings. Information passes between them, so nobody enters it twice.
Can we start with just one tool?
Yes. Most businesses start with Ailo to handle social media and messaging, or with Bandla to stop missed bookings. You can add the other tools when you are ready.
Does it replace our existing CRM or tools?
It depends on what you have. GPTify OS includes ILDAM, a clean CRM for service businesses, but it can also pass bookings, contacts and notes into 1C, Google Calendar or your existing database.
Who owns our customer data?
You do. Your customer data runs on your own accounts or servers, is encrypted at rest and in transit, and is never used to train public AI models.
How long does setup take?
A standard setup for one location or team takes 5 to 10 working days, including connecting your channels, loading your procedures and training your staff.
